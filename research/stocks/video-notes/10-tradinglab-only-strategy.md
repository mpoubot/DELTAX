## The three steps

### 1 — Market structure, with a real twist

The useful idea: **not every swing low is a valid low.** A low becomes
structurally valid only once price has broken the *previous high*. Traders who
flip bearish on any low break are reacting to an invalid level and fighting an
intact trend.

**Rule:** in an uptrend, only a break of the *validated* low flips the bias.

Precise enough to code, and genuinely distinct from naive structure-break logic.

### 2 — Supply and demand zones

Demand zones in uptrends, supply in downtrends; trade only with the established
trend. **Construction:** find consolidation immediately preceding a sharp
impulsive move, mark low-to-high of the last candle before that move. Entry on
re-entry into the zone, stop just outside, target the recent swing extreme.

### 3 — Reward-to-risk filter

**Reject setups below 2.5:1**, even when steps 1 and 2 both pass.

A third independent source arriving at a payoff-ratio floor — Graystone 2:1,
Cameron 2:1, this 2.5:1. The number varies; the principle is unanimous.
