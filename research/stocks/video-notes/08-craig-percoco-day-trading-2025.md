## The convergence that matters

A UK forex educator (video 07) and a US crypto day trader independently arrive at
the same risk process. Neither cites the other.

| Idea | V07 | V08 |
|---|---|---|
| Expectancy over win rate | explicit formula | R-multiples; 30%-win-rate example still profitable |
| Size from the stop | risk ÷ stop distance | risk ÷ (entry − stop) |
| Fixed risk per trade | 1% of account | fixed dollar amount |
| Test → demo → live | backtest → demo → live | bar replay → simulated → real |
| Log every trade | full schema | tracker with win %, averages |

## Trading psychology — three reframes

The strongest original content here:

1. **Losing is not inherently bad.** Losses are the cost of being in a position
   where you can be right.
2. **Being wrong is not bad.** Worked example: 10 trades, 7 losses at −1R (−7R),
   3 wins at +5.2R, +2.5R, +3.1R (+10.8R) → **net +3.8R while wrong 70% of the
   time.**
3. **Making money on a trade does not make it a good trade.** Outcome and process
   are separate. An unquantified win is one decision from giving it all back.

Point 3 has no equivalent in the options bucket, where every source judged trades
purely by whether they paid. **For an automated agent it survives translation
intact: judge the process, never the individual outcome.**

## Strategy development loop

Concept (from observation) → explicit rule set → evaluate (frequency, average win,
average loss) → keep or discard. Then: bar replay → simulated account → live.

Demonstrates on a 3-trade sample and — to his credit — immediately says that's far
too small.

## Technical content

Trendlines and structure breaks · **break-and-retest** entries · Fibonacci 50%
and 61.8% · **fair value gaps** (three candles where wick 1 and wick 3 don't
overlap; entry at gap midpoint) — mechanically defined and genuinely testable.

## Flags

- **The actual signal is paywalled.** He states he can't reveal what the buy/sell
  indicator computes. **The demonstrated strategy is not reproducible** — only the
  risk framework around it is.
- **Lead generation throughout** — custom indicators, trackers, a private team,
  gated behind a DM.
- **The Fibonacci justification is numerology** — seashells, plants, facial
  symmetry. Not a market mechanism. If 61.8% works it's because traders watch it;
  test it as a self-fulfilling reference, not a natural law.
- **Unverified outcome claims:** $3,000 in two hours risking $500; $7,500 across
  four or five trades. No statements, no sample.
- **Leverage presented as access, not risk.** 10× to cut required capital, with no
  mention of liquidation.
- He does show a losing trade, which is more than most of the options bucket.
